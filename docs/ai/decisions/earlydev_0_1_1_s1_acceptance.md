## Work packages and acceptance

These are proposed implementation packages, not started tasks or passing tests.
Add their executable backlog tasks once the S1 design choices are settled.

| Order | Deliverable | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reproduce/fix inherited defects through the normal launcher; add actual-path regressions. | MC-AI-0477/0478/0479 raw failures, diagnosed causes, regressions and ordinary-play evidence; no content-free replacement snapshot. |
| 2 | Define seeded generation/configuration, H/S/latitude conventions and solid block identities. | Configuration/ID/codec rejection tests; exact column cases; periodicity and generator-version tests. |
| 3 | Compare/select a tectonic simulation or approximation, then calibrate it and optional detail. | Seeded plate/crust/motion and base-height previews; varied continent sizes, ranges/foothills, trenches, islands and peninsulas; measured initialization/memory/query cost; land-area and determinism checks. |
| 4 | Integrate materialized chunks, production streaming, collision and material-aware surface/LOD rendering. | Shared-sample agreement; chunk/seam/refinement/codec cases; source-bound top/side/cutaway visuals in the normal renderer. |
| 5 | Establish safe spawn and qualify normal movement through the new world. | Ordinary walking/flight, turns, fresh terrain, both climate boundaries, wrapping and x200; final-package gameplay and platform evidence. |

Start from the existing `WorldGeneration`, `WorldGenerationScheduler`, `Chunk`,
`SparseWorld` and `HeightTileSurfaceMesher` suites; extend relevant production
codec/collision tests rather than duplicating implementation expressions.
Useful negative cases include unknown materials, mismatched generation identity,
out-of-range heights, S-4/S-5 shoreline mistakes, band-boundary off-by-one,
request-order dependence and regeneration after eviction.

Inspect several predeclared seeds before accepting the geography. Show plate
and crust structure, motion/boundary classification, tectonic base heights,
optional elaboration, final block heights and materials. With post-tectonic
detail disabled, previews must already show coherent large-scale landforms;
seeded stochastic initial crust remains allowed. Check relations between
tectonics and relief, not just whether a final image looks plausible. Include
continents of different sizes, trenches, islands and peninsulas across the
corpus; do not require every seed to contain an identical catalog. Provisional
same-class checks include ridges/rifts and island arcs; decide their exact
coverage during model selection rather than inventing new gameplay features.
Use normal-game traversals as well as maps. Preserve rejected previews and
parameter sets. Preview images
and maps are implementation deliverables; none were generated by this planning task.

The final S1 candidate must also retain the inherited Mac performance contract:
normal launcher, HUD on, 120 Hz display, radius 128, target 120 fresh gameplay
FPS with at least 99.8% of measured moving time at >=119. Freeze actual
framebuffer pixels, FOV, render/LOD quality, permissions and hardware, and
predeclare the raw-timestamp/window accounting protocol. Report ordinary travel,
turns, new terrain, border traversal and x200 separately. Inspect full-viewport
near-first terrain service during motion. Do not substitute stationary FPS,
repeated presentation, neutral-input queue drainage or a center-only tile probe.

Keep normal-package owner acceptance or a scoped exception separate from
automated checks. Preserve the handoff's Windows-secondary/untouched restriction;
the generic release policy's later full-Windows default needs reconciliation
against that owner restriction before qualification, without starting Windows
work from this plan. Existing applicable Mac/Android and analysis checks still
apply; this document claims no runtime or platform pass.

## Decisions for the next planning pass

- Review the proposed H/S boundary convention, fixed submerged ground band,
  normalized-Y latitude definition and 45–55% validation target.
- Compare simplified tectonic evolution and procedural tectonic approximation;
  select from base-height/landform previews and measured bounded costs. Specify
  the plate/crust model, atlas/detail scales and seed corpus, with deterministic
  arithmetic/quantization and source/build constraints in view. Continental
  scale is seed-driven rather than a fixed “few” or “many” landmass preset.
- Specify the compact material/configuration wire contract and material-aware
  cliff/LOD representation. Keep behavior measurable at the production path.
- Set concrete material appearance for grass top/sides, dirt, bedrock, snow
  and ice; no slipperiness, melting or transparent solid ice is presumed here.

These open design choices do not authorize implementation. At implementation
start, initialize the version-specific three-hour PDF/heartbeat lifecycle in
[RELEASE_REPORTS.md](../RELEASE_REPORTS.md); planning starts neither the timer
nor a reporting automation.
