## Geography and map review

Use seeds **0, 1, 42, 777, 2004, 8800555** in that order. Export initial crust,
plate/motion/strain, tectonic heights, shaped bed/water topology, climate and
actual solid materials. Preserve source/configuration identity and rejected
attempts. The standalone map command uses the gameplay generator before any
GPU initialization; no in-game map UI is required in S1.

Inspect smooth, irregular and ragged coasts; elongated islands with satellites,
large-island groups, archipelagos and isolated/subcontinental islands. Check
uneven land concentration and sparse unusual features. Inspect peninsulas, connected ranges/foothills,
trenches/ridges, lakes and large rivers across the corpus. Do not hardcode the
same feature catalog in each seed. Fjord/harbour masks are diagnosed geometric
candidates with stated limits, not proofs of glacial history or navigability.
Noise-free *post-tectonic* comparisons must already have large geography;
stochastic initial crust remains allowed. Check component causality, not only
attractive final images. The temporary experiment provides actual maps and
measurements; [its report](earlydev_0_1_1_s1_experiments.md) states their limits.

## Retained gameplay contract

The final S1 candidate retains the inherited Mac target: normal launcher,
HUD on, 120 Hz display, radius 128, target 120 fresh gameplay FPS with at least
99.8% of moving time at ≥119. Freeze actual framebuffer pixels, FOV,
render/LOD quality, permissions and hardware, and predeclare raw-timestamp/window
accounting. Report ordinary travel, turns, newly streamed terrain, borders and
x200 separately. Inspect the full viewport during motion. Stationary FPS,
repeated presentation or a centre-only queue probe cannot establish this.

Cursor startup/focus/refocus/exit, gravity/jump/flight/passthrough, multiplayer
world identity and late streaming remain representative checks. Offscreen
screenshots supplement ordinary play. Require applicable Mac/Android acceptance;
Windows remains secondary/untouched under the standing owner restriction.
Report missing platform evidence separately. Exact-package owner acceptance or
a scoped exception remains distinct from passing automation and publication.

clean S7 claim was invalid. No prototype timing closes that gap. At later game
implementation start, initialize the mandatory version-specific three-hour
PDF/heartbeat lifecycle in [RELEASE_REPORTS.md](../RELEASE_REPORTS.md). Research
and temporary model experiments start neither that timer nor an automation.
